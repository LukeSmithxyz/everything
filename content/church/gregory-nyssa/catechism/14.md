"Then why," it is asked, "did the Deity descend to such humiliation? Our faith
is staggered to think that God, that incomprehensible, inconceivable, and
ineffable reality, transcending all glory of greatness, wraps Himself up in the
base covering of humanity, so that His sublime operations as well are debased
by this admixture with the grovelling earth."
