# Kontakion 1

From your youth, you loved our Savior Jesus Christ, Holy One of God. Seeking only Him with fervor to follow Him in fasting and prayer always, struggling in the desert with a heart full of humility and acquiring the love of God, you showed yourself to be a chosen vessel of theology about the mercy of the Creator and toward all His creation. For this we pray to you, intercede for us, Holy Father, with your prayers pleasing to God, that we may sing to you: Rejoice, Holy Father Isaac the Syrian, great intercessor for the whole world!

# Ikos 1

Human by nature, angelic in your conduct, you were seen, Holy Father Isaac the Syrian, and therefore, marveling at your life pleasing to God, we say to you:

Rejoice, Father, that Christ has revealed you to us; \
Rejoice, you who entered the monastery of the Great Matthew as a young man together with your brother; \
Rejoice, for you show us the way to our hearts; \
Rejoice, discoverer of divine mysteries; \
Rejoice, you who teach us to accept with humility and patience the bitter medicine of temptations; \
Rejoice, for you are our example of holy asceticism; \
Rejoice, guide of Christians toward repentance; \
Rejoice, you who teach us that no one has ascended to heaven without the cross; \
Rejoice, inexhaustible fountain of tears and holy joy; \
Rejoice, outpouring of amazing miracles; \
Rejoice, for you nourish us with your prayers; \
Rejoice, for from early childhood you chose the good deed; \
Rejoice, Saint Isaac, chosen by Christ to be the guide of monks; \
Rejoice, high humility, for you fled the honor of abbotship and let your brother receive this honor before the brothers of the monastery; \
Rejoice, great intercessor for all mankind and for all creation; \
Rejoice, for in our trials and tribulations you intercede for us before the Holy Trinity; \
Rejoice, for you have chosen good deeds and diligence as your companions; \
Rejoice, O Most Reverend One, spiritual trumpet that proclaims divine love to all creation; \
Rejoice, for you have shown us the word of the true faith; \
Rejoice, Holy Father Isaac the Syrian, great intercessor for the whole world!

# Kontakion 2

The eyes of your soul reaching out to Christ, the Light of all, and despising all worldly worship, you followed the light of God’s commandments through hardship, for He called you to sing to Him unceasingly: Alleluia!

Rejoice, lover of harsh fasting; \
Rejoice, you who teach us to love and suffer with the poor; \
Rejoice, intercessor for all creation; \
Rejoice, for your heart was filled with mercy; \

Rejoice, bearer of God; \

Rejoice, great guide to the understanding of God’s mercy; \
Rejoice, for you taught us to increase mercy in our souls; \
Rejoice, for you clothed yourself in humility as in a bright garment; \
Rejoice, you who taught us that humility is God’s wonderful garment; \
Rejoice, Holy Father Isaac the Syrian, great intercessor for the whole world!

# Kontakion 3

The light of grace shone brightly upon you as a servant of the Word, Saintly Abba, you were filled without burning by the fire of God, and you warmed the souls of the powerless. For the gift of virtues, we sing to God with holy joy: Alleluia!

# Ikos 3

For your wonderful life and deeds, Father Isaac, wise in God, you were chosen as bishop of Nineveh, but not wanting to stay too long in the city, you withdrew again to the sweet wilderness, praying for all and for the salvation of all. Therefore, seeing the image of your humility and gentleness, we say with love:

Rejoice, you who were made bishop of Nineveh for the holiness of your life; \
Rejoice, for seeing the cruelty of the inhabitants and the disobedience of the city, you wept because of the longing for solitude; \
Rejoice, for you were deemed worthy of the gift of righteous judgment; \
Rejoice, great lover of the desert; \
Rejoice, profound singer of grace; \
Rejoice, for you have become the bearer of the light of understanding; \
Rejoice, contemplation of the living word; \
Rejoice, for you turn hearts to simplicity; \
Rejoice, discoverer of hidden mysteries; \
Rejoice, for you give good thoughts to the humble; \

Rejoice, for you have left us much light of divine thoughts in your writings; \
Rejoice, torch lit by the fire of the Holy Spirit; \
Rejoice, for with wisdom from above you were a good guide to monks; \
Rejoice, unquenchable light in the darkness of this world; \
Rejoice, great saint of Syria who gave the Church of Christ many martyrs for the glory of God; \
Rejoice, joy of the righteous believers; \
Rejoice, ray of sunshine of Christ, the Sun of Righteousness; \
Rejoice, illuminator and preacher of the divine word; \
Rejoice, Holy Father Isaac the Syrian, great intercessor for the whole world!

# Kontakion 4

Like Abraham, who became a friend of God, so you, holy one, have become holy through your love for all, and like Isaac, you have been likened to sacrifice, obedience, and blessing, through which you have brought glory and thanksgiving to the Holy Trinity, singing ceaselessly with your heart: Alleluia!

Rejoice, bearer of holy joy; \
Rejoice, you who rejoice with the angels in heaven and with all the saints; \
Rejoice, torch of the desert and light of those whose souls are deserted; \
Rejoice, lofty spiritual life that is difficult to attain; \
Rejoice, golden pen of the Holy Spirit; \
Rejoice, source of joy for angels and men; \
Rejoice, in whom the light of the Holy Trinity shone; \
Rejoice, for you followed the spiritual struggles of the fathers of the desert; \
Rejoice, ascetic of unceasing prayer and living humility; \
Rejoice, gentle light of the desert who shows us the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit shining in boundless love; \
Rejoice, Holy Father Isaac the Syrian, great intercessor for the whole world!

# Kontakion 5

You have taught us through your writings, holy one of God, to flee from the sadness inspired by the devil who always wants to throw us into despair because of our sins, telling us that the deceiver’s effort is to convince all people that God does not care for them, but we should never believe his cunning deception, but have all our hope in God’s goodness and love for all His creation, because true repentance is not born of fear but of tasting God’s merciful forgiveness, singing to Him always: Alleluia!

# Ikos 5

How wonderful we find in your writings, holy Father Isaac the Syrian, about the mysteries of God, showing that God’s gift is the true bond that can unite us with Him, and not threats and fear of punishment, but understanding God must start not from our smallness but from His greatness. We, the unworthy, rejoice in all your holy teachings and with love we say to you:

Rejoice, for you did not desire exaltation before men; \
Rejoice, easy conqueror of the cunning serpent; \
Rejoice, for you took Christ as your helper and savior; \
Rejoice, good father, for you left us examples to follow; \
Rejoice, joy of spiritual people; \
Rejoice, for you guide our lives toward salvation; \
Rejoice, for your love for God was so great that you prayed even for the fallen angels; \
Rejoice, strengthener of prayer; \
Rejoice, tireless guide to spiritual things; \
Rejoice, healer of despair; \
Rejoice, comforter of passions; \
Rejoice, intercessor for salvation; \
Rejoice, mediator for the oppressed; \
Rejoice, destroyer of evil; \
Rejoice, corrector of the wrongdoers; \
Rejoice, you who strengthened us with prayer; \
Rejoice, you who calm the powerful; \
Rejoice, you who labored in silence and solitude; \
Rejoice, you who taught us to always trust in God’s care; \
Rejoice, Holy Father Isaac the Syrian, great intercessor for the whole world!

# Kontakion 6

If we always remember your words, Holy One, in which you told us that God is righteous precisely because he is merciful, we would dare more to run with humility and repentance before the All-Forgiving One to receive forgiveness for our sins and abundant Grace, singing together with you to the merciful God an unceasing: Alleluia!

# Ikos 6

It is difficult for people to comprehend the spiritual and profound meanings you have revealed to us about God’s inexpressible love when you said: “Through love He brought the world into existence, through love He guides it in its existence in time, through love He will lead it to that wonderful transformation, and through love the world will be swallowed up in the great mystery of the One who accomplished all these things,” and we, daring, say to you with humility and love:

Rejoice, you who received wisdom from God; \
Rejoice, you who gave holy teaching to men; \
Rejoice, you who were chosen to bring peace; \
Rejoice, you who proclaim Christ; \

Rejoice, for retreating into the wilderness of Mount Mataot; \
Rejoice, pearl polished by fasting, prayer, and the study of Holy Scripture in the wilderness of the anchorites; \
Rejoice, spiritual eye, beholder of goodness; \
Rejoice, height of love, who interceded in prayer even for demons; \
Rejoice, star shining from the harshness of the desert; \
Rejoice, cedar grown on the mountain of faith; \
Rejoice, you who have ascended to spiritual heights; \
Rejoice, joy of Christ the Lord; \

# Kontakion 7

We have learned from your writings that to see unceasingly God’s care for all the works of His hands is true spiritual vision, and that the mind of man will then be overwhelmed with wonder and amazement, throwing us face down in prayer and saying: Alleluia!

Rejoice, you who attained peace of mind and heart; \

# Kontakion 1

From your youth, you loved our Savior Jesus Christ, Holy One of God. Seeking only Him with fervor to follow Him in fasting and prayer always, struggling in the desert with a heart full of humility and acquiring the love of God, you showed yourself to be a chosen vessel of theology about the mercy of the Creator and toward all His creation. # Prayer of Saint Isaac the Syrian

Lord Jesus Christ, our God, Who searches Your creation, To Whom my passions and the weakness of our human nature and the strength of our enemy are revealed, You Yourself cover me from his wickedness, for his power is strong, and our nature is passionate and our strength is weak. But You, O Good One, Who know our weakness, Who bear the burden of our weakness, protect me from the turmoil of my thoughts and the flood of my passions, and make me worthy of this holy service of Yours, lest, in my defiled desires, I lose its sweetness and find myself without shame and without fear before You. But, Lord, my sweet Jesus, have mercy on me and save me.

# Prayer of Repentance by Saint Isaac the Syrian

Lord Jesus Christ, our God, Who wept for Lazarus and shed tears of sorrow and mercy for him, accept my tears. With Your passions, heal my passions. With Your wounds, heal my wounds. With Your blood, cleanse my blood and mix in my body the fragrance of Your life-giving body.

The gall with which Your enemies fed You, sweeten the bitterness with which my adversary has fed me. Your body stretched out on the Cross, stretch out to You my mind, which is pulled down by the devils. May Your head, which You bowed on the Cross, lift up my head, which has been struck by my adversaries. May Your most holy hands, nailed to the Cross by the lawless, draw me to You from the abyss of perdition, as Your most holy mouth has promised. Your face, mocked with slaps and spittle, fill with radiance my face, defiled by iniquity. Your Spirit, which You entrusted to the Father when You were on the Cross, guide me to You, through Your grace. I do not have a heart full of sorrow to seek You. I have no repentance or humility, which return sons to their inheritance.

I have no comforting tears, Master. My mind has been darkened by worldly things and cannot seek You with sorrow. My heart has grown cold from so many temptations and cannot be warmed by tears of love for You. But You, Lord Jesus Christ, God, Treasurer of goodness, grant me unblemished repentance and a sorrowful heart, that I may set out with all my soul in search of You, for without You I will be estranged from all good. Therefore, give me, O Good One, Your grace. May the Father, Who begot You from His bosom without years and before all ages, renew in me the image of Your icon. I have forsaken You, Lord, do not forsake me. I have strayed from You, come out in search of me. Take me to Your spiritual pasture. Count me among the sheep of Your chosen flock. Feed me together with them from the greenery of Your divine Mysteries, for their pure hearts are Your dwelling place, and the radiance of Your revelations is seen in them. Your radiance is the comfort and rest of those who have labored for
You in tribulations and all kinds of torments. Grant this radiance to me, the unworthy, through the grace and love for mankind of our Savior Jesus Christ, forever and ever. Amen.
