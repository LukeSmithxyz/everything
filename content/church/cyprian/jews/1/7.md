¶ 7. Also that they should lose the Light of the Lord.

In Isaiah: "Come ye, and let us walk in the light of the Lord. For He hath sent
away His people, the house of Israel."[^1] In His Gospel also, according to
John: "That was the true light which lighteth every man that cometh into this
world. He was in this world, and the world was made by Him, and the world knew
Him not."[^2] Moreover, in the same place: "He that believeth not is judged
already, because he hath not believed in the name of the only begotten Son of
God.  And this is the judgment, that light is come into the world, and men
loved darkness rather than light."[^3]




[^1]: Isa. ii. 5, 6.
[^2]: John i. 9, 10.
[^3]: John iii. 18, 19.
