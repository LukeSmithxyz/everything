¶ 14. That the old pastors should cease and new ones begin.

In Ezekiel: "Wherefore thus saith the Lord, Behold, I am above the shepherds;
and I will require my sheep from their hands, and I will turn them away from
feeding my sheep; and they shall feed them no more, and I will deliver my sheep
from their mouth, and I will feed them with judgment."[^1] In Jeremiah the Lord
says: "And I will give you shepherds according to my own heart, and they shall
feed you with the food of discipline."[^2] In Jeremiah, moreover:  "Hear the
word of the Lord, ye nations, and tell it to the islands which are afar off.
Say, He that scattereth Israel will gather him, and will keep him as a shepherd
his flock: for the Lord hath redeemed Jacob, and taken him out from the hand of
him that was stronger than he."[^3]




[^1]: Ezek. xxxiv. 10–16.
[^2]: Jer. iii. 15.
[^3]: Jer. xxxi. 10, 11.
