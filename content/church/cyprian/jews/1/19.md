¶ 19. That two peoples were foretold, the elder and the younger; that is, the old
people of the Jews, and the new one which should consist of us.

In Genesis: "And the Lord said unto Rebekah, Two nations are in thy womb, and
two peoples shall be separated from thy belly; and the one people shall
overcome the other people; and the elder shall serve the younger."[^1] Also in
Hosea: "I will call them my people that are not my people, and her beloved that
was not beloved. For it shall be, in that place in which it shall be called not
my people, they shall be called the sons of the living God."[^2]




[^1]: Gen. xxv. 23.
[^2]: Hos. ii. 23; i. 10.
