¶ 18. That in the last times the same mountain should be manifested, and upon it
the Gentiles should come, and on it all the righteous should go up.

In Isaiah: "In the last times the mountain of the Lord shall be revealed, and
the house of God upon the tops of the mountains; and it shall be exalted above
the hills, and all nations shall come upon it, and many shall walk and say,
Come, and let us go up into the mountain of the Lord, and into the house of the
God of Jacob; and He will tell us His way, and we will walk in it.  For from
Sion shall proceed the law, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem; and He
shall judge among the nations, and shall rebuke much people; and they shall
beat their swords into ploughshares, and their spears into pruning-hooks, and
they shall no more learn to fight."[^1] Also in the twenty-third Psalm: "Who
shall ascend into the hill of the Lord, or who shall stand in His holy place?
He that is innocent in his hands, and of a clean heart; who hath not received
his life in vanity, and hath not sworn craftily to his neighbour. He shall
receive the blessing from the Lord, and mercy[^2] from the God that saveth him.
This is the generation of those who seek Him, that seek the face of the God of
Jacob."[^3]




[^1]: Isa. ii. 2–4.
[^2]: "Misericordiam."
[^3]: Ps. xxiv. 3–6.
