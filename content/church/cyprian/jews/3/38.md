¶ 38. That the servant of God ought to be innocent, lest he fall into secular
punishment.

In the Epistle of Paul to the Romans:  "Wilt thou not be afraid of the power?
Do that which is good, and thou shalt have praise of it."[^1]




[^1]: Rom. xiii. 3.
