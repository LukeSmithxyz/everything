¶ 109. That the sick are to be visited.[^1]

In Solomon, in Ecclesiasticus: "Be not slack to visit the sick man; for from
these things thou shalt be strengthened in love."[^2] Also in the Gospel: "I
was sick, and ye visited me; I was in prison, and ye came unto me."[^3]



[^1]: [Elucidation XII. See p. 528, supra.]
[^2]: Ecclus. vii. 39.
[^3]: Matt. xxv. 36.
