¶ 94. That the Eucharist is to be received with fear and honour.[^1]

In Leviticus: "But whatever soul shall eat of the flesh of the sacrifice of
salvation, which is the Lord's, and his uncleanness is still upon him, that
soul shall perish from his people."[^2] Also in the first to the Corinthians: "
Whosoever shall eat the bread or drink the cup of the Lord unworthily, shall be
guilty of the body and blood of the Lord."[^3]




[^1]: [Note, not to be worshipped, but received.]
[^2]: Lev. vii. 20.
[^3]: 1 Cor. xi. 27.
