¶ 9. That brethren ought to support one another.

To the Galatians: "Each one having others in consideration, lest ye also should
be tempted. Bear ye one another's burdens, and so ye shall fulfil the law of
Christ."[^1]




[^1]: Gal. vi. 1, 2.
