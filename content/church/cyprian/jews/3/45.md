157940Ante-Nicene Fathers/Volume V Vol. V, Cyprian, The Treatises of Cyprian,

¶ 45. That hope is of future things, and therefore that our faith concerning
those things which are promised ought to be patient.

In the Epistle of Paul to the Romans:  "We are saved by hope. But hope that is
seen is not hope; for what a man seeth, why doth he hope for? But if we hope
for what we see not, we hope[^1] for it in patience."^[2]




[^1]: Some read" exspectamus," "we wait for it."
[^2]: Rom. viii. 24, 25.
