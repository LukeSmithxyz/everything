¶ 99. That judgment will be according to the times, either of equity before the
law, or of law after Moses.

Paul to the Romans: "As many as have sinned without law, shall perish without
law; and as many as have sinned in the law, shall be judged also by the law."^[
1]




[^1]: Rom. ii. 12.
