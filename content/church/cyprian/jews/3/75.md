¶ 75. That every person ought to have care rather of his own people, and
especially of believers.

The apostle in his first Epistle to Timothy:  "But if any take not care of his
own, and especially of those of his own household, he denies the faith, and is
worse than an infidel."[^1] Of this same thing in Isaiah: "If thou shalt see
the naked, clothe him; and despise not those who are of the household of thine
own seed."[^2] Of which members of the household it is said in the Gospel: "If
they have called the master of the house Beelzebub, how much rather them of his
household!"[^3]




[^1]: 1 Tim. v. 8.
[^2]: Isa. lviii. 7.
[^3]: Matt. x. 25.
