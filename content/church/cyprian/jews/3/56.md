56.  That nothing that is done is hidden from God.

In the Wisdom of Solomon: "In every place the eyes of God look upon the good
and evil."[^1] Also in Jeremiah: "I am a God at hand, and not a God afar off.
If a man should be hidden in the secret place, shall I not therefore see him?
Do not I fill heaven and earth? saith the Lord."[^2] Also in the first of
Kings: "Man looketh on the face, but God on the heart."[^3] Also in the
Apocalypse: "And all the churches shall know that I am the searcher of the
reins and heart; and I will give to every one of you according to his works."^[
4] Also in the eighteenth Psalm: "Who understands his faults? Cleanse Thou me
from my secret sins, O Lord."[^5] Also in the second Epistle of Paul to the
Corinthians: "We must all be manifested before the tribunal of Christ, that
every one may bear again the things which belong to his own body, according to
what he hath done, whether good or evil."[^6]




[^1]: Prov. xv. 3.
[^2]: Jer. xxiii. 23, 24.
[^3]: 1 Sam. xvi. 7.
[^4]: Rev. ii. 23.
[^5]: Ps. xix. 12.
[^6]: 2 Cor. v. 10.
