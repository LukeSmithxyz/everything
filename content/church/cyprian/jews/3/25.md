¶ 25. That unless a man have been baptized and born again, he cannot attain unto
the kingdom of God.

In the Gospel according to John:  "Except a man be born again of water and the
Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God. For that which is born of the
flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit."[^1] Also in
the same place: "Unless ye eat the flesh of the Son of man, and drink His
blood, ye shall not have life in you."[^2]




[^1]: John iii. 5, 6.
[^2]: John vi. 53.
