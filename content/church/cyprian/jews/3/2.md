¶ 2. In works and alms, even if by smallness of power less be done, that the will
itself is sufficient.

In the second Epistle of Paul to the Corinthians: "If there be a ready will, it
is acceptable according to what a man hath, not according to that which he hath
not; nor let there be to others a mitigation, but to you a burdening.[^1]




[^1]: 2 Cor. viii. 12, 13.
