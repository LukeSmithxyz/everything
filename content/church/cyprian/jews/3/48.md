¶ 48.  That we must not take usury.

In the thirteenth Psalm:[^1] "He that hath not given his money upon usury, and
has not received gifts concerning the innocent. He who doeth these things shall
not be moved for ever."[^2] Also in Ezekiel: "But the man who will be
righteous, shall not oppress a man, and shall return the pledge of the debtor,
and shall not commit rapine, and shall give his bread to the hungry, and shall
cover the naked, and shall not give his money for usury."[^3] Also in
Deuteronomy: "Thou shalt not lend to thy brother with usury of money, and with
usury of victuals."[^4]




[^1]: The Oxford edition has "the fourteenth." [Elucidation XIII.]
[^2]: Ps. xv. 6.
[^3]: Ezek. xviii. 7, 8.
[^4]: Deut. xxiii. 19.
