¶ 72. That servants, when they have believed, ought to serve their carnal masters
the better.

In the Epistle of Paul to the Ephesians:  "Servants, obey your fleshly masters
with fear and trembling, and in simplicity of your heart, as to Christ; not
serving for the eye, as if you were pleasing men; but as servants of God."[^1]




[^1]: Eph. vi. 5, 6.
