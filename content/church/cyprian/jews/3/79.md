79.  That innocency asks with confidence, and obtains.

In the Epistle of John: "If our heart blame us not, we have confidence towards
God; and whatever we ask, we shall receive from Him."[^1] Also in the Gospel
according to Matthew: "Blessed are they of a pure heart, for they shall see
God."[^2] Also in the twenty-third Psalm: "Who shall ascend into the hill of
the Lord? or who shall stand in His holy place? The innocent in hands and of a
pure heart."[^3]




[^1]: 1 John ii. 21, 22.
[^2]: Matt. v. 8.
[^3]: Ps. xxiv. 3, 4.
