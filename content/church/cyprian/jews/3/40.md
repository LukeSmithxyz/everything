157935Ante-Nicene Fathers/Volume V Vol. V, Cyprian, The Treatises of Cyprian,

40.  That we must not labour noisily nor boastfully.

In the Gospel according to Matthew:  "Let not thy left hand know what thy right
hand doeth, that thine alms may be in secret; and thy Father, which seeth in
secret, shall render to thee."[^1] Also in the same place:  "When thou doest an
alms, do not sound a trumpet before thee, as the hypocrites do in the streets
and in the synagogues, that they may be glorified of men. Verily I say unto
you, They have fulfilled their reward."[^2]




[^1]: Matt. vi. 3, 4.
[^2]: Matt. vi. 2.
