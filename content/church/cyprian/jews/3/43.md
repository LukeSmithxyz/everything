157938Ante-Nicene Fathers/Volume V Vol. V, Cyprian, The Treatises of Cyprian,

¶ 43. That he who believes can immediately obtain (i.e., pardon and peace).

In the Acts of the Apostles: "Lo, here is water; what is there which hinders me
from being baptized?  Then said Philip, If thou believest with all thine heart,
thou mayest."[^1]




[^1]: Acts viii. 36, 37.
