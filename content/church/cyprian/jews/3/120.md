¶ 120. That we are to be urgent in prayers.

In the Epistle of Paul to the Colossians:  "Be instant in prayer, and watch
therein."[^1] Also in the first Psalm: "But in the law of the Lord is his will,
and in His law will he meditate day and night."[^2]




[^1]: Col. iv. 2.
[^2]: Ps. i. 2. The Oxford edition continues: "Likewise in Solomon; 'Be not
    hindered from praying ever, and delay not unto death to be justified; for
    the repayment of the Lord abideth for ever.'" [In a day when there were few
    Bibles, and no printed books, no concordances, and no published collections
    of this sort, reflect on the value of this treatise to a young believer,
    and on the labour of his pastor in making it.]
