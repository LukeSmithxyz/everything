¶ 55. That we must not please men, but God.

In the fifty-second Psalm: "They that please men are confounded, because God
hath made them nothing."[^1] Also in the Epistle of Paul to the Galatians: "If
I wished to please men, I should not be the servant of Christ."[^2]




[^1]: Ps. liii. 5.
[^2]: Gal. i. 10.
