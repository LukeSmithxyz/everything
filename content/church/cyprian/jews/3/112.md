¶ 112. That those are more severely judged, who in this world have had more
power.

In Solomon: "The hardest judgment shall be made on those who govern. For to a
mean man mercy is granted; but the powerful shall suffer torments mightily."^[1
] Also in the second Psalm: "And now, ye kings, understand; be amended, ye who
judge the earth."[^2]




[^1]: Wisd. vi. 6.
[^2]: Ps. ii. 10.
