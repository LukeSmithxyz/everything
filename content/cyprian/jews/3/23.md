¶ 23. That evil is not to be returned for evil.

In the Epistle of Paul to the Romans:  "Rendering to no man evil for evil."[^1]
Also in the same place:  "Not to be overcome of evil, but overcome evil with
good."[^2] Of this same thing in the Apocalypse: "And He said unto me, Seal not
the words of the prophecy of this book; because now the time is at hand. And
let those who persist in hurting, hurt: and let him who is filthy, be filthy
still: but let the righteous do still more righteousness: and in like manner,
let him that is holy do still more holiness. Behold, I come quickly; and my
reward is with me, to render to every man according to his deeds."[^3]




[^1]: Rom. xii. 17.
[^2]: Rom. xii. 21.
[^3]: Rev. xxii. 10–12.
