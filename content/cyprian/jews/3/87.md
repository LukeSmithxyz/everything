87.  That believers ought to be simple, with prudence.

In the Gospel according to Matthew:  "Be ye prudent as serpents, and simple as
doves."[^1] And again: "Ye are the salt of the earth. But if the salt have lost
his savour, in what shall it be salted? It is good for nothing, but to be cast
out abroad, and to be trodden under foot of men."[^2]




[^1]: Matt. x. 16.
[^2]: Matt. v. 13.
