¶ 63. That the sin of fornication is grievous.

In the first Epistle of Paul to the Corinthians: "Every sin whatsoever a man
doeth is outside the body; but he who committeth fornication sinneth against
his own body. Ye are not your own, for ye are bought with a great price.
Glorify and bear the Lord in your body."[^1]




[^1]: 1 Cor. vi. 18–20.
