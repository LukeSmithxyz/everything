¶ 73. Moreover, that masters should be the more gentle.

Also in the same place: "And, ye masters, do the same things to them,
forbearing anger: knowing that both your Master and theirs is in heaven; and
there is no choice of persons with Him."[^1]




[^1]: Eph. vi. 9.
