¶ 33. That the Father judgeth nothing, but the Son; and that the Father is not
glorified by him by whom the Son is not glorified.

In the Gospel according to John: "The Father judgeth nothing, but hath given
all judgment unto the Son, that all may honour the Son as they honour the
Father. He who honoureth not the Son, honoureth not the Father who hath sent
Him."[^1] Also in the seventy-first Psalm: "O God, give the king Thy judgment,
and Thy righteousness to the king's son, to judge Thy people in righteousness."
[^2] Also in Genesis: "And the Lord rained upon Sodom and Gomorrah sulphur, and
fire from heaven from the Lord."[^3]




[^1]: John v. 22, 23.
[^2]: Ps. lxxii. 1, 2.
[^3]: Gen. xix. 24.
