¶ 24. That it is impossible to attain to the Father but by His Son Jesus Christ.

In the Gospel according to John: "I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man
cometh unto the Father, but by me."[^1]  Also in the same place: "I am the
door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved."[^2]




[^1]: John xiv. 6.
[^2]: John x. 9.
