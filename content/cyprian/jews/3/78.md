¶ 78. That we must not speak with heretics.

To Titus: "A man that is an heretic, after one rebuke avoid; knowing that one
of such sort is perverted, and sinneth, and is by his own self condemned."[^1]
Of this same thing in the Epistle of John: "They went out from among us, but
they were not of us; for if they had been of us, they would doubtless have
remained with us."[^2] Also in the second to Timothy: "Their word doth creep as
a canker."[^3]




[^1]: Tit. iii. 10, 11.
[^2]: 1 John ii. 19.
[^3]: 2 Tim. ii. 17.
