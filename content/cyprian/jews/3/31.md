31.  That he who does not believe is judged already.

In the Gospel according to John: "He that believeth not is already judged,
because he hath not believed in the name of the only[^1] Son of God. And this
is the judgment, that light has come into the world, and men have loved
darkness rather than light."[^2] Of this also in the first Psalm: "Therefore
the ungodly shall not rise up in judgment, nor sinners in the council of the
righteous."[^3]




[^1]: Unice; but some read unigeniti, "only-begotten."
[^2]: John iii. 18, 19.
[^3]: Ps. i. 5.
