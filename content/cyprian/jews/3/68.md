157963Ante-Nicene Fathers/Volume V Vol. V, Cyprian, The Treatises of Cyprian,

¶ 68. That we must depart from him who lives irregularly and contrary to
discipline.

Paul to the Thessalonians: "But we have commanded you, in the name of Jesus
Christ, that ye depart from all brethren who walk disorderly, and not according
to the tradition which they have received from us."[^1] Also in the forty-ninth
Psalm: "If thou sawest a thief, at once thou rannest with him, and placedst thy
portion with the adulterers."[^2]




[^1]: 2 Thess. iii. 6. [A very noteworthy safeguard of apostolic ordinances;
    but mark the charity with which it is softened, 2 Thess. iii. 14, 15.
    Compare also cap. ii. 15.]
[^2]: Ps. l. 28.
