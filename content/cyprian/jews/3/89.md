¶ 89. That the end of the world comes suddenly.

The apostle says: "The day of the Lord shall so come as a thief in the night.
When they shall say, Peace and security, then on them shall come sudden
destruction."[^1] Also in the Acts of the Apostles: "No one can know the times
or the seasons which the Father has placed in His own power."[^2]




[^1]: 1 Thess. v. 2, 3.
[^2]: Acts i. 7.
