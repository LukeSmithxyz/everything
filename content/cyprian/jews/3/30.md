¶ 30. That what any one has vowed to God, he must quickly repay.

In Solomon: "According as thou hast vowed a vow to God, delay not to pay it."^[
1] Concerning this same thing in Deuteronomy: "But if thou hast vowed a vow to
the Lord thy God, thou shalt not delay to pay it: because the Lord thy God
inquiring shall seek it of thee; and it shall be for a sin. Thou shalt observe
those things that shall go forth out of thy lips, and shalt perform the gift
which thou hast spoken with thy mouth."[^2] Of this same matter in the
forty-ninth Psalm: "Sacrifice to God the sacrifice of praise, and pay thy vows
to the Most High.  Call upon me in the day of trouble, and I will deliver thee,
and thou shalt glorify me."[^3] Of this same thing in the Acts of the Apostles:
"Why hath Satan filled thine heart, that thou shouldst lie to the Holy Ghost,
when thy estate was in thine own power? Thou hast not lied unto men, but unto
God."[^4] Also in Jeremiah: "Cursed is he who doeth the work of God
negligently."[^5]




[^1]: Eccles. v. 4.
[^2]: Deut. xxiii. 21–23.
[^3]: Ps. l. 14, 15.
[^4]: Acts v. 3, 4.
[^5]: Jer. xlviii. 10.
