¶ 66. That the discipline of God is to be observed in Church precepts.

In Jeremiah: "And I will give to you shepherds according to my own heart; and
they shall feed the sheep, feeding them with discipline."[^1] Also in Solomon,
in the Proverbs: "My son neglect not the discipline of God, nor fail when
rebuked by Him. For whom God loveth, He rebuketh."[^2] Also in the second
Psalm: "Keep discipline, lest perchance the Lord should be angry, and ye perish
from the right way, when His anger shall burn up quickly against you. Blessed
are all they who trust in Him."[^3] Also in the forty-ninth Psalm: "But to the
sinner saith God, For what dost thou set forth my judgments, and takest my
covenant into thy mouth? But thou hatest discipline, and hast cast my words
behind thee."[^4] Also in the Wisdom of Solomon: "He who casteth away
discipline is miserable."[^5]




[^1]: Jer. iii. 15.
[^2]: Prov. iii. 11, 12.
[^3]: Ps. ii. 12.
[^4]: Ps. l. 16.
[^5]: Wisd. iii. 11.
