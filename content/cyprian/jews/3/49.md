¶ 49. That even our enemies must be loved.

In the Gospel according to Luke: "If ye love those who love you, what thank
have ye? For even sinners love those who love them."[^1] Also according to
Matthew: "Love your enemies, and pray for those who persecute you, that ye may
be the children of your Father who is in heaven, who maketh His sun to rise
upon the good and the evil, and giveth rain upon the righteous and the
unrighteous."[^2]




[^1]: Luke vi. 32.
[^2]: Matt. v. 44, 45.
