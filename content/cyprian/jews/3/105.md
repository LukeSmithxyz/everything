¶ 105. That they are frequently to be corrected who do wrong in domestic duty.

In Solomon: "He who spareth the rod, hateth his son."[^1] And again: "Do not
cease from correcting the child."[^2]




[^1]: Prov. xiii. 24.
[^2]: Prov. xix. 18.
