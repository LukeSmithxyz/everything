¶ 114. That one ought to make confession while he is in the flesh.

In the fifth Psalm: "But in the grave who will confess unto Thee?"[^1] Also in
the twenty-ninth Psalm: "Shall the dust make confession to Thee?"[^2] Also
elsewhere that confession is to be made: "I would rather have the repentance of
the sinner than his death."[^3] Also in Jeremiah: "Thus saith the Lord, Shall
not he that falleth arise? or shall not he that is turned away be converted?"^[
4]




[^1]: Ps. vi. 5. [Here, as often, the grave is represented as enjoying a
    temporary victory, for the flesh is no longer capable of worship. Not till
    the whole man is restored comes 1 Cor. xv. 54, 55.]
[^2]: Ps. xxx. 9.
[^3]: Ezek. xxxiii. 11.
[^4]: Jer. viii. 4.
