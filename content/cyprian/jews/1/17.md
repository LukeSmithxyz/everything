¶ 17. That the old priesthood should cease, and a new priest should come, who
should be for ever.

In the cixth Psalm: "Before the morning star I begat thee. The Lord hath sworn,
and He will not repent, Thou art a priest for ever, after the order of
Melchizedek."[^1] Also in the first book of Kings, God says to the priest Eli: 
"And I will raise up to me a faithful priest, who shall do all things which are
in my heart: and I will build him a sure house; and he shall pass in the
presence of my anointed ones for all days. And it shall be, whosoever shall
remain in thine house, shall come to worship for an obolus of money, and for
one loaf of bread."[^2]




[^1]: Ps. cx. 3.
[^2]: 1 Sam. ii. 35, 36.
