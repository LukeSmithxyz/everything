¶ 9. That the former law which was given by Moses was to cease.

In Isaiah: "Then shall they be manifest who seal the law, that they may not
learn; and he shall say, I wait upon the Lord, who turneth away His face from
the house of Jacob, and I shall trust in Him."[^1] In the Gospel also:  "All
the prophets and the law prophesied until John."[^2]




[^1]: Isa. viii. 16, 17.
[^2]: Matt. xi. 13.
