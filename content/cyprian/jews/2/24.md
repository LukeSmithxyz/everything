¶ 24. That He was not to be overcome of death, nor should remain in Hades.

In the twenty-ninth Psalm: "O Lord, Thou hast brought back my soul from hell."^
[1] Also in the fifteenth Psalm: "Thou wilt not leave my soul in hell, neither
wilt Thou suffer Thine Holy One to see corruption."[^2] Also in the third
Psalm:  "I laid me down and slept, and rose up again, because the Lord helped
me."[^3] Also according to John: "No man taketh away my life from me; but I lay
it down of myself. I have the power of laying it down, and I have the power of
taking it again. For this commandment I have received from my Father."[^4]




[^1]: Ps. xxx. 3.
[^2]: Ps. xvi. 10.
[^3]: Ps. iii. 5.
[^4]: John x. 18.
