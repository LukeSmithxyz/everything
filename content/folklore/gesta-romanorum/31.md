We read, that at the death of Alexander, a golden sepulchre was constructed,
and that a number of philosophers assembled round it. One said—"Yesterday,
Alexander made a treasure of gold: and now gold makes a treasure of him."
Another observed—"Yesterday, the whole world was not enough to satiate his
ambition; to-day, three or four ells of cloth are more than sufficient." A
third said—"Yesterday, Alexander commanded the people; to-day the people
command him." Another said—"Yesterday, Alexander could enfranchise thousands;
to-day he cannot free himself from the bonds of death." Another
remarked—"Yesterday, he pressed the earth; to-day it oppresses him."
"Yesterday," continued another, "All men feared Alexander; to-day men repute
him nothing." Another said, "Yesterday, Alexander had a multitude of friends;
to-day, not one." Another said, "Yesterday, Alexander led on an army; to-day,
that army bears him to the grave."


# Application.

My beloved, any one may be called Alexander who is rich and worldly-minded; and
to him may the observations of the philosophers be truly applied.
