In the reign of the emperor Henry II., a certain city was besieged by its
enemies. Before they had reached its walls, a dove alighted in the city, around
whose neck a letter was suspended which bore the following inscription. "The
generation of dogs is at hand; it will prove a quarrelsome breed; procure aid,
and defend yourselves resolutely against it."


# Application.

My beloved, the dove is the Holy Spirit, which thus descended on Christ.
